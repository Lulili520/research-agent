# CCF venue rules

Use these rules whenever CCF scope or labels affect retrieval or reporting.

## Source of truth

- Verify against the current official CCF recommended international conference and journal catalog at `https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/By_category/`.
- Record the catalog edition, official page URL, and access date. The catalog changes; never use a memorized category as the sole basis.
- Prefer the official CCF domain/category page when resolving venue acronyms, renames, mergers, or category disputes.

## Interpretation

- Record CCF domain and A/B/C category as venue metadata.
- Do not translate venue category into paper quality or evidence strength. Assess each paper from its contribution and evidence.
- CCF's conference scope covers Full paper or Regular paper. Short, Demo, Technical Brief, Summary, Findings, Workshop, and colocated-event papers are not automatically covered by the main venue's category.
- Verify the actual track and paper type from proceedings or the official conference program.

## Venue identity

Record:

- canonical venue name and acronym;
- conference edition and publication year;
- main conference, journal track, findings, workshop, demo, short, dataset/benchmark, or other track;
- proceedings publisher and stable paper identifier;
- CCF catalog edition, domain, and category;
- official proceedings URL;
- related OpenReview, arXiv, project, code, dataset, and later journal version links.

Conference dates and proceedings publication years can differ; keep both when relevant. Treat venue renames and merged conferences explicitly rather than normalizing them silently.
